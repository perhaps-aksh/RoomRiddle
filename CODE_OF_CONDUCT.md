# Code of Conduct

Room Riddle follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted.

To report unacceptable behaviour, open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/perhaps-aksh/RoomRiddle/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
